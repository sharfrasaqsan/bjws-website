#### **<mark>1. Foreword</mark>**

It is with great humility and sincerity that we present this profile of the **Buhary (Janaza) Funeral Welfare Society** , a community-based organization dedicated to serving the Muslim residents of the Buhary Jummah Mosque area. Since its founding in 2023, our Society has strived to provide essential services with dignity, especially in moments of grief and loss. Our mission has been simple yet profound: **to stand with our community when they need us the most.**

##### **2. Introduction**

The **Buhary (Janaza) Funeral Welfare Society** (BJWS) was founded by a group of socially conscious individuals who recognized the need for a structured, respectful, and supportive funeral service within the Buhary Mosque area. As a close-knit Muslim community, the need for a collective approach to funeral arrangements, cemetery maintenance, and social assistance was both timely and vital.

From its humble beginnings, the Society has grown into a trusted institution. By integrating Islamic principles, community participation, and modern organizational practices, BFS has made significant strides in serving the people with transparency and care.

#### **<mark>3. Vision & Mission</mark>**

### Our Vision

To be the leading community funeral support service in the region, upholding Islamic values, compassion, and dignity for every individual.

### Our Mission

- **To provide efficient and respectful funeral services.**

- **To maintain a clean and accessible cemetery.**

- **To offer dry ration and Welfare support to the needy.**

- **To create a network of active and committed members.**

- **To promote unity and brotherhood within the community** .

#### **<mark>4. Our Core Values</mark>**

- **Compassion:** Every life matters, and every loss is respected.

- **Service:** We serve not for profit, but for the pleasure of Allah.

- **Integrity:** Transparent operations and sincere efforts define us.

- **Unity:** Our strength is in our togetherness.

- **Accountability:** Every member has a voice and every rupee is accounted for.

##### **5. Historical Background**

Officially Register The year **2023** marked a significant turning point for the Buhary Mosque community. Observing the logistical difficulties families faced during funerals—ranging from preparation of the deceased to burial procedures and cemetery management—a small team of community elders and youth came together to form a structured organization.

With collective consultation ( _shura_ ), the **Buhary (Janaza) Funeral Welfare Society** was officially formed. A membership drive was launched, a basic constitution was drafted, and initial contributions were collected. This foundational year saw the Society take on its first few funeral arrangements, setting the tone for its future.

#### **<mark>6. Structure & Membership</mark>**

#### **Organizational Structure**

The Society operates under the following structure:

- **President**

- **Vice President**

- **Secretary**

- **Vice Secretary**

- **Treasurer**

- **Executive Committee Members**

- **Volunteers**

#### **Membership Details**

To promote affordability and inclusivity, the Society has a **dual payment system** :

- **Monthly Contribution:** LKR 50

- **Annual Contribution:** LKR 500

Members are required to fill out a **Membership Form** containing personal and emergency contact information, family details, and preferred communication method. Each member is given an ID number and their contribution is recorded and maintained transparently.

#### **<mark>7. Activities & Programs</mark>**

#### **A. Funeral Services**

The cornerstone of our mission. Our services include:

- Arranging **janazah prayers**

- Ghusl and kafan (ritual washing and shrouding)

- Transporting the deceased

- Assisting family with legal and burial formalities

- Ensuring respectful and timely burials

##### **B. Cemetery Maintenance**

Our dedicated **cemetery clean-up team** regularly:

- Clears overgrown plants and waste

- Ensures pathways are clean

- Maintains signage and grave markers

- Coordinates with local authorities for infrastructure improvements

##### **C. Monthly Dry Ration Distribution**

In response to economic hardship among families, especially widows and orphans, we distribute **dry ration packages** monthly. These include rice, dhal, sugar, flour, tea, and other essentials.

##### **D. Awareness and Training**

We conduct **Islamic funeral education workshops** for youth and volunteers, covering:

- Proper funeral rites

- Emotional support to grieving families

- Hygiene and safety protocols

##### **8. Achievements (2023–2025)**

##### **Funeral Management**

- **28 funeral arrangements** completed with full community support

- Coordinated with **local Imams and health authorities** for timely burials

##### **Membership Growth**

- From **25 founding members** to over **300 active members**

- Introduced digital record-keeping for membership and payments

##### **Cemetery Upkeep**

- Cleared and renovated 1.5 acres of cemetery land

- Installed new **water facilities and shade tents** for visitors

##### **Social Assistance**

- Over **750 dry ration packs** distributed

- Assisted **7 financially struggling families** with funeral costs

##### **Community Events**

- Held 4 **Janazah preparation workshops**

- Conducted 2 **volunteer appreciation programs**

#### **<mark>9. Community Impact</mark>**

The Buhary (Janaza) Funeral Welfare Society is now recognized as a **pillar of support** in the Buhary Mosque area. Families no longer have to navigate funerals alone. Grieving members are supported emotionally, logistically, and financially.

Furthermore, our clean and serene cemetery is a source of pride and a spiritual reminder of our duty toward the deceased. The monthly dry ration program has helped uplift dignity among the needy, while our growing membership reflects rising trust in our mission.

#### **<mark>10. Future Goals</mark>**

##### **Short-Term Goals (Next 12 Months)**

- Expand dry ration support to include **Ramadan hampers**

- Establish a **small funeral fund** for emergency expenses

- Recruit and train **20 more youth volunteers**

- Launch a **mobile app** for member updates and funeral notifications

##### **Long-Term Vision (Next 3–5 Years)**

- Build a **dedicated ghusl room facility** near the cemetery

- Purchase a **funeral transport van**

- Extend membership to surrounding communities

- Form a **Ladies Wing** for widow and orphan support

##### **11. Financial Overview**

##### **Sources of Income**

- Membership fees (monthly and annual)

- Individual donations and sadaqah

- End-of-life contributions

- Fundraising events (e.g., Iftar drives, charity sales)

##### **Expenses**

- Funeral supplies (shrouds, transportation)

- Cemetery maintenance (labor, tools)

- Dry ration purchases

- Administrative costs (records, forms, communication)

##### **Transparency**

- All transactions are recorded in a **monthly ledger**

- Year-end financial reports are shared with all members

- Audits conducted by a **community-nominated treasurer**

##### **12. Conclusion**

The **Buhary (Janaza) Funeral Welfare Society** is more than just a service provider — it is a **symbol of unity** , **care** , and **responsibility** . Guided by the principles of Islam and driven by a commitment to community welfare, we have turned moments of difficulty into opportunities for collective strength and compassion.

We thank every member, donor, volunteer, and leader who has contributed to our journey so far. May Allah (SWT) continue to accept our humble efforts and guide us toward even greater service.
